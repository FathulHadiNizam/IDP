# Integrated Design Project – Scooter Lawn Mower (Group E + Group G)

This repository documents the Integrated Design Project (IDP) carried out by **Group E + Group G**, consisting of 10 members under the supervision of **Prof. Madya Dr. Mohd Juzaila Bin Abd. Latif**. I, **Muhammad Fathul Hadi** from Group E, served as the **Team Leader** for this project. The project focused on the design, development, and fabrication of a **Scooter Lawn Mower**, aiming to combine user mobility, ergonomics, and cutting efficiency into one practical prototype.
https://idp-portfolio.webnode.page/

---

## Project Overview
The Scooter Lawn Mower was designed to address limitations of conventional mowers by improving maneuverability, reducing user fatigue, and enhancing grass-cutting performance. The project followed a systematic engineering design process:

- Problem identification & objectives setting  
- Concept generation and selection  
- Detailed design and CAD modelling (SolidWorks)  
- Engineering analysis (ANSYS)  
- Application of DFMA and FMEA  
- Fabrication, assembly, and testing  

---

## Key Contributions & Leadership
- **Team Leadership**: Led a 10-member team (Group E + Group G), delegated tasks, monitored progress, and ensured timely milestone delivery.  
- **Technical Oversight**: Supervised CAD modelling, finite element analysis, and design validation using DFMA and FMEA.  
- **Fabrication Management**: Directed manufacturing processes including cutting, welding, wiring, and assembly.  
- **Problem-Solving**: Guided the team to overcome challenges such as motor overheating, blade efficiency, and traction issues on grass surfaces.  

---

## Technical Scope
- **Design Tools**: SolidWorks 2024 for 3D modelling, ANSYS for stress/thermal analysis.  
- **Manufacturing Processes**: Cutting, welding, grinding, drilling, wiring, and assembly.  
- **Engineering Applications**: DFMA, FMEA, ergonomic considerations, and performance testing.  
- **Testing & Validation**: Evaluated prototype performance on real grass surfaces, ensuring safety, durability, and efficiency.  

---

## Project Outcomes
- Successfully developed a working prototype of the **Scooter Lawn Mower**.  
- Achieved mowing efficiency of **2,582 m²/hour**.  
- Enhanced ergonomics by reducing operator fatigue with seated operation.  
- Demonstrated integration of design, analysis, and fabrication in a real-world project.  
- Strengthened teamwork, leadership, and project management skills across a multidisciplinary team.
