# Integrated Design Project – Scooter Lawn Mower (Group E + Group G)

This repository documents the Integrated Design Project (IDP) carried out by **Group E + Group G**, consisting of 10 members under the leadership of **Muhammad Fathul Hadi** (Group G). The project focused on the design, development, and fabrication of a **Scooter Lawn Mower**, aiming to combine user mobility, ergonomics, and cutting efficiency into one practical prototype.

---

## Project Overview
The Scooter Lawn Mower was designed to address limitations of conventional mowers by improving maneuverability, reducing user fatigue, and enhancing grass-cutting performance. The project followed a systematic engineering design process:

- Problem identification & objectives setting  
- Concept generation and selection  
- Detailed design and CAD modelling (SolidWorks)  
- Engineering analysis (ANSYS)  
- Application of DFMA and FMEA  
- Fabrication, assembly, and testing  

---

## Key Contributions & Leadership
- **Team Leadership**: Coordinated a 10-member team, managed task delegation, monitored progress, and ensured timely milestone completion.  
- **Technical Oversight**: Supervised CAD design, finite element analysis, and design validation using DFMA and FMEA.  
- **Fabrication Management**: Led manufacturing processes including cutting, welding, wiring, and assembly.  
- **Problem-Solving**: Guided the team in resolving issues such as motor overheating, blade efficiency, and traction challenges.  

---

## Technical Scope
- **Design Tools**: SolidWorks 2024 for 3D modelling, ANSYS for stress/thermal analysis.  
- **Manufacturing Processes**: Cutting, welding, grinding, drilling, wiring, and assembly.  
- **Engineering Applications**: DFMA, FMEA, ergonomic considerations, and performance testing.  
- **Testing & Validation**: Evaluated prototype on real grass surfaces, monitoring efficiency, safety, and durability.  

---

## Project Outcomes
- Successfully produced a working prototype of the **Scooter Lawn Mower**.  
- Achieved mowing efficiency of **2,582 m²/hour**.  
- Enhanced ergonomics by reducing user walking fatigue through seated operation.  
- Demonstrated full-cycle integration of design, analysis, and fabrication.  
- Strengthened teamwork, leadership, and project management skills across a multidisciplinary team.
